## Custom Template & Bespoke Design Service

If a client wants a unique design that is not in the catalog, SayWith offers **100% Bespoke Custom Templates**:

### What Can Be Customized:
1. **Interactive Animations**: Custom particles (floating hearts, sakura blossoms, snowflakes, autumn leaves, or confetti).
2. **Audio & Media Layouts**: Custom audio visualizers, interactive lyrics synchronization, multiple photo carousels, or video chapters.
3. **Special Occasion Themes**:
   - **Weddings & Proposals**: Custom couple monogram, wedding color palette, RSVP button, or photo timeline.
   - **Memorials & Tributes**: Gentle candle animations, acoustic playback, memory book format.
   - **Corporate & Events**: Brand colors, official logos, speaker videos, event agendas.
4. **Interactive Secret Messages**: Tap-to-reveal love letters, scratch-off reveals, or countdown timers to an anniversary or birthday.

## How to Configure Screenshots and Videos for Each Template

In [`public/data/templates.json`](file:///c:/Users/Admin/Desktop/PROJECTS/SayWith_System/SayWith/public/data/templates.json), each template has the following structure:

```json
{
  "id": "Template1",
  "num": 1,
  "name": "Cinematic Immersion",
  "category": "Minimalist & Modern",
  "desc": "Full-screen portrait video with floating modern typography and overlay controls.",
  "tag": "Popular",
  "accent": "from-blue-600 to-indigo-900",
  "previewType": "image",
  "previewUrl": "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/your-username/your-repo/main/screenshots/template-1.png",
  "posterUrl": ""
}
```

- To display a mobile screenshot: Set `"previewType": "image"` and set `"previewUrl"` to your GitHub raw link or image URL.
- To display a short looping video preview: Set `"previewType": "video"`, set `"previewUrl"` to your `.mp4` / `.webm` link, and optionally provide a `"posterUrl"`.
- The template cards automatically adapt to the **9:16 mobile phone ratio** with full-screen preview enabled!
